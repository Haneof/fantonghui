## Scope accepted

M0-021 freezes the legal TaskState and EventStatus transition matrices in production code and exposes read-only transition queries plus revision-level transition validators.

Task canary requirements are satisfied: `RUNNING -> WAITING_RESULT` is legal and `COMPLETED -> RUNNING` is illegal. All terminal Task states (`COMPLETED`, `FAILED`, `EXPIRED`, `CANCELLED`) have no outgoing transitions.

Event canary requirements are satisfied: `CANDIDATE -> REJECTED` is legal and `MERGED -> ACTIVE` is illegal. `MERGED` and `SPLIT` are terminal in the frozen transition matrix.

State changes are explicitly validated as append-only object-history steps: `validate_task_revision_transition` and `validate_event_revision_transition` require the same object_id, exactly `revision + 1`, and an allowed state transition. This prevents a transition helper from treating mutation of the current durable revision as a valid state change.
