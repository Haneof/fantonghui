## Chief Engineer review

M0-019 is accepted after independent inspection of the taskbook, the actual production store implementation, the exact base/head diff, existing M0-006 reference tests, new focused M0-019 tests, and exact-head GitHub Actions.

The material defect found during review was a public `validate_references=False` argument on `SQLiteWorldStore.commit()`. Although normal writes validated references, a caller could intentionally bypass the durable reference-integrity boundary. M0-019 removes that switch, making recursive ObjectRef/SourceRef validation mandatory for every world commit.

The accepted implementation preserves previously frozen semantics:
- pinned refs resolve exact revisions;
- floating refs remain generic latest-visible navigation semantics;
- target visibility is bounded by the referencing object's learned_at knowledge cutoff;
- legal same-transaction references are supported through the pending set;
- ordinary mutual semantic links are not rejected merely because they form a cycle;
- current-revision self-citation is rejected with DEPENDENCY_INVALID;
- prior-revision self-history links remain legal;
- missing/not-yet-visible targets fail with NOT_FOUND before durable writes;
- invalid refs are never silently deleted or repaired.
